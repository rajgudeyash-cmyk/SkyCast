SkyCast

Smart weather forecasts for any city in the world · Version {{ app_version }}

What you get

  • Live conditions: temperature, feels-like, humidity, wind, pressure, clouds, rain, UV, sunrise and sunset
  • Hourly forecast for the next 24 hours
  • 7-day forecast with highs, lows and UV index
  • Search any city worldwide, or use your current location
  • Favorite cities and recent searches
  • °C / °F switch and light / dark themes
  • Installs on your home screen and opens like a native app

Where the data comes from

All weather data and city lookups come from Open-Meteo.com, a free weather API, used under the CC BY 4.0 licence. SkyCast never makes up weather: if the service can't be reached, you'll see an error or clearly labelled saved data.

Open-Meteo's free service is for non-commercial use.

Add to your home screen

  1. Open SkyCast in Chrome on your Android phone.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right).
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Confirm. The SkyCast icon now appears with your other apps.

Privacy

Your theme, units, favorites and recent searches are saved only on this device (browser storage). Your location is used only when you tap the location button, and only to ask Open-Meteo for the weather there. SkyCast has no accounts and no ads.
